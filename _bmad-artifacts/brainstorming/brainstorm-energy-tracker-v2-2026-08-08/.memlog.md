- (idea by user) Tariff optimization becomes first-class: actively find more attractive tariff conditions to save cost, not just record the current tariff

- (idea by coach) Weekly voice recap doubles as the insight-review ritual itself: app asks 'anything unusual this week?' and threads the answer directly onto the spike it already flagged
- (technique) started Job to Be Done
- (idea by user) Real emotional job: detect early whether consumption is trending significantly above baseline (fear = surprise invoice); ~100 kWh over baseline is a felt 'magic number' worth actively surfacing
- (idea by user) Three emotional states on opening the app: within anticipated range = relief/all good; below baseline = delight; over ~100kWh threshold = actionable worry
- (idea by user) Second job: open the app to decide whether to switch tariffs, not just to check status
- (idea by user) Decision screen: green/red badge for tariff attractiveness shown both with and without switching bonus factored in
- (idea by user) Relative market standing indicator: 'your tariff is top 10% / mid-range / expensive end' compared to available market tariffs
- (insight by user) Real-world constraint: user is a tenant, not property owner — smart main meter is unlikely/complicated; accepts irregular 1-2 day manual reading cadence as the honest baseline, not a flaw to engineer away
- (insight by user) Spreadsheet's real weakness vs a purpose-built app: integration (combining external data/online searches) and mobile typing friction — not the tabular entry itself
- (idea by user) User is a developer and wants the tool to 'reach spheres' the spreadsheet can't — implies appetite for an extensible/programmable platform, not just a fixed feature set
- (idea by coach) Zero-tap ambient artifact: single push notification 'Quiet week, 240kWh under pace, Saturday gaming binge already absorbed' — no chart, no numbers, dismissed in one swipe
- (decision by user) Adopted the ambient zero-tap notification as the concrete north-star artifact for v2's future interaction model
- (decision by coach) Affinity clustering run on the 20 candidates: 5 clusters — Pattern Detective, Tariff Savings Radar, Context Capture (voice+AI), Extensible Platform, Real-World Constraints (foundation)

Context Capture specifically targets appliances that can't be measured (induction cooktop, bathroom water heater)

- (decision by coach) MoSCoW confirmed — Must: Pattern Detective, Real-World Constraints. Should: Context Capture (text/tap first), Tariff Savings Radar (bonus-decay-aware, no market ranking). Could: Extensible Platform, Voice-mode input. Won't this round: market-percentile/tariff-comparison-wizard
- (insight) Synthesis: v2 core direction is Pattern Detective (gap-tolerant rolling baseline over Main Meter + imported Smart Plug signal) surfaced as one glanceable status culminating in a zero-tap ambient notification. Context Capture (voice-first, AI-plausibility-assisted) is Decomposition's heir for unmeasurable built-ins (induction cooktop, water heater), not a generic log. Tariff Savings Radar shares its 'decaying baseline' math with the pace threshold. Extensible Platform is 3 scoped points (custom event/plausibility rules, generic data-source mapping, tunable thresholds) — voice is just an input modality on point 1, not a separate feature. Market-percentile tariff comparison confirmed as deliberate long-run deferral (needs 3rd-party data feed). Foundation: tenant, no smart meter, 1-2 day irregular cadence accepted as ground truth.
