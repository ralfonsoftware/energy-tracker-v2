The real emotional job is detecting early whether consumption is trending significantly above baseline — the fear is a surprise invoice. About 100 kWh over baseline is a felt "magic number" worth actively surfacing.

Three emotional states on opening the app: within anticipated range = relief, all good; below baseline = delight; over the ~100 kWh threshold = actionable worry.

06
Synthesis

The North Star, Assembled

Where every thread lands.

Session insight

v2's core direction is the Pattern Detective — a gap-tolerant rolling baseline over the Main Meter plus imported Smart Plug signal — surfaced as one glanceable status, culminating in the zero-tap ambient notification.

Context Capture (voice-first, AI-plausibility-assisted) is Decomposition's heir for unmeasurable built-ins — the induction cooktop, the water heater — not a generic log.

The Tariff Savings Radar shares its "decaying baseline" math with the pace threshold.

Extensible Platform is three scoped points: custom event/plausibility rules, generic data-source mapping, tunable thresholds — voice is just an input modality on point one, not a separate feature.

Market-percentile tariff comparison is confirmed as a deliberate long-run deferral, needing a 3rd-party data feed.

Foundation: tenant, no smart meter, 1–2 day irregular cadence — accepted as ground truth.
