Deep Warm Hybrid — Dark & Light

Deep Warm Hybrid keeps Deep Glass's structure — stacked rear/front panels for real z-depth, a diagonal specular sweep, and bold 700-weight type — and re-skins it with Warm Glass's amber/copper/sage palette and porch-light mark, so the instrument feels confident but domestic rather than clinical. Glass reads very differently on white than on black, so the light variant trades glowing highlights for frosted-white translucency and soft warm-tinted drop shadows: the same panel stack and sheen survive, but status dots get a gentle lift-shadow instead of a halo, and all three semantic colors are darkened/desaturated to hold contrast on cream instead of glowing against near-black.
