Settings + Tariff Radar — wide-column follow-up (2026-09-28)

Follow-up to key-trend-history.html's 660px → 900px column update. Ralf's screenshot of Settings' Rooms/Power Points/Devices tree showed a long single-column list of mostly one-line collapsed rows; this file answers "how do we use 900px better" for Settings and, per the same question, for Tariff Radar.

New patterns this session:
  • Rooms/Power Points grid-with-expand: collapsed Room/Power Point rows sit in a repeat(auto-fill, minmax(220px,1fr)) grid; opening one spans it to full width (grid-column: 1 / -1) and reveals its children below — same accordion interaction, just reflowed. Device rows stay a plain list (they carry a drag-reorder handle — a 2D grid makes "move" ambiguous).
  • Section pairing: AI Plausibility Check + Household sit side by side (both short, unrelated, previously alone on a wide row). Yearly Baseline and the Rooms card stay full width.
  • Tariff Radar: the Tariff History list (no drag handle) gets the identical entry-grid treatment as Meter Readings/Events. The Compare form's current-vs-candidate summary panels move from stacked to side by side; the verdict card stays full width below as the shared conclusion.
