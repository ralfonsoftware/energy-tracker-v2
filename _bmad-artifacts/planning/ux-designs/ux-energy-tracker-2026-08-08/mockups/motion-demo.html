Motion Demo — Green Eco, Dark

Motion here reinforces "calm and trustworthy," not "responsive and snappy." Every move is slow, soft, and settles rather than bounces: the status card eases to rest with no overshoot, the specular highlight drifts rather than flashes, and the primary action compresses into the glass instead of flashing a color change on press — this is a household utility reporting a number, not a game giving feedback. All motion is gated behind prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference; with reduced motion requested, every element renders directly in its finished state and interactions become instant, undamped state changes — confirmed both in code (see the two @media blocks below) and in the illustrated panel to the right.

Watch it loop: the phone below replays an 8-second cycle continuously — status card entrance → specular sweep → primary-action press → sheet opens → sheet closes — so it's watchable without clicking anything. The "Log reading" button's press state is also real: hover/focus/click it directly (:active / :focus-visible) and the auto-demo pauses so you can inspect the pressed state on your own terms.

What's animating, and why

  • Status card entrance/settle — opacity + scale (0.96→1) + an 8px translateY, eased out with no bounce. Reads as "the number has arrived and is safe to trust," not "look at me."
  • Specular sweep — a soft diagonal highlight drifts once across the glass over ~2.2s, opacity-ramped at both ends so it never hard-cuts in or out.
  • Primary-action press — "+ Log reading" compresses to 0.965 scale and its shadow pulls in, like glass being pushed down into the stack. No color flash.
  • Sheet open — Log Reading slides up from the bottom edge while the dashboard backdrop blurs in behind it, then reverses on close.
