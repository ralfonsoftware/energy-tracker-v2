Trend History — merged Meter Readings placement

Story 4.1 / Story 4.3 — the one open UX question from the Epic 3 Retro's Significant Discovery: where Story 2.8's Meter Reading list/edit surface lands once it's folded into Trend History instead of staying a standalone page. Page title also carries the new Smart Plug Import entry icon (FR-4 amendment, 2026-08-26) — the second of the two entry points, opening the same shared screen as the Dashboard's. See key-smart-plug-import.html.

Placement: a third card, "Meter Readings," sits between the trend chart and the Room → Power Point → Device tree — collapsed by default (same disclosure idiom as the tree below it). Chart and Meter Readings are two views of the same Main Meter data (FR-8) and read as a pair; the tree is a structurally different signal (FR-9, Smart Plug, AD-14) and stays visually separate, last on the page. Row content, pagination, the Pending flag, and the correction note are carried over unmodified from Story 2.8's shipped page.
