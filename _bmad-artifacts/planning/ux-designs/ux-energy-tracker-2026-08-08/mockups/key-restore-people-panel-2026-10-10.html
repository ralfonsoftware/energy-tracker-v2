Restore result: “People in this backup” and the invite action

Story 11.1 (AD-25). After a restore, DataImportPanel shows who was in the backup and offers the existing invite action. Built as-is, the invite action is InviteGeneratePanel — itself a glass card (max-w-sm) — dropped inside the import card, which reads as card-in-card (Option A, what was seen live). Three ways to resolve it, each rendered in Dark and Light, at ≥660px (column 704px) and 400px, idle and with a generated link. Tokens are copied from wide-settings-tariff-2026-09-28.html; no new design language.

Constraints that shape the options
  • Card hierarchy (DESIGN/colors.md): a card with an interactive control takes the glass tier. Here the whole import card is already that glass card, so the invite control should read as content of it (a button, a field), not a second card.
  • Density (your earlier preferences): compact controls, no wordy blocks; one tap to the result.
  • Don’t restyle InviteGeneratePanel globally: Settings uses it unchanged. B needs only an opt-in prop (e.g. bare) that skips the card wrapper; the default render stays identical.
  • Copy is the real en-US strings; the de-DE strings are ~15% longer (checked live: the hint wraps to the same two lines at ≥660px).
Option AAccept the nesting (current build)

The invite card sits inside the import card, left-aligned at max-w-sm (384px).

Pros
Zero extra work; reuses the component untouched.
Cons
Card-in-card at every width; at ≥660px a narrow card hugging the left of a wide one with empty space beside it; the inner backdrop-blur draws a darker rectangle (annotated) in the dark theme; at 400px the two paddings (18px + 22px) squeeze the link field.

Recommendation: Option B

  • It is the only option that removes card-in-card at both widths; C leaves the phone case unchanged and A is the problem itself.
  • It keeps the restore-result moment on one surface: read who was in the backup, press one button, copy the link. No modal hop.
  • Implementation is small and safe: InviteGeneratePanel gains an opt-in bare prop that renders its content in a plain div instead of GlassCard; DataImportPanel passes it. Settings keeps the existing card and InviteMemberRow is not touched.
  • If you would rather keep Settings’ exact pattern, pick C and accept that phones still show the nested card.
