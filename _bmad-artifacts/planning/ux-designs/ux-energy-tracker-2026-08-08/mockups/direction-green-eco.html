Green Eco — Dark & Light

Green as brand, green as status are deliberately two separate token sets. Less energy is greener for the planet and lighter on the bill, so one teal-leaning green anchors the interactive chrome — the mark, links, active states — and appears nowhere else. Status keeps its own triad tuned to stay legible against that chrome: a cool, desaturated sage for "within range" (calm, nothing to do), a distinctly more saturated grass-emerald for "below baseline" — allowed to be the single greenest thing on screen, since it's the good news — and a warm amber, never red, for "trending," so urgency reads through warmth rather than alarm. Dark and Light are equal citizens: identical panel-stack z-depth, specular sweep and bold type in both, with Light trading glow for frosted-white translucency and soft green-tinted shadows to fake the same depth on a bright ground.
