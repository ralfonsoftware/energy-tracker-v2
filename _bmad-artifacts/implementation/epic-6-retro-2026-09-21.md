## What Went Well

- **The AD-14 isolation guard test paid off exactly as designed.** `PatternDetectiveDoesNotReferenceSmartPlugOrEventDataTests` was written during Story 6.1, in anticipation of Story 6.3's Event-aware correlation logic, before any Event-aware code existed to protect against. It was re-verified unmodified after both 6.1 and 6.3 — a guard test built ahead of the feature that would need it.
- **The Epic 5 retro's "first real consumer" bet on `IAiPlausibilityClient`/AD-8 landed clean**, same shape as Epic 5's own `AuditCorrection`/`BonusDecayNormalizer` bets from Epic 4. The no-op-when-unconfigured path was live-verified end to end (toggle on, unconfigured backend, background job completes cleanly with no correlation, nothing else in the product branches on AI availability).
- **Ambiguity was consistently flagged for review rather than silently resolved** — 6.1's tag data-model shape, 6.3's AD-8 local-vs-cloud tension, the correlation window/threshold choice, the correlation storage shape — each was reasoned through, explicitly disclosed as a design decision, and left open for review rather than guessed and buried. This discipline held across all three stories.
- **Both hotfixes inside the window went through documented specs**, not undocumented direct fixes — the Epic 4 retro's hard gate is now proven across two consecutive epics.
- **Scope discipline on Story 6.1 held even under pressure to finish**: rather than force AC #4 through on a technicality, review correctly ruled the display half unsatisfied and spun up a follow-up story instead.

## What Didn't Go Well / Patterns

1. **A dev-story task got checked off before it was actually run.** 6.2's Task 7 (live verification, AC #5 empty state) was marked complete on the strength of a component test alone, contradicting the task's own "does not defer" instruction — caught during code review, not before. Ralf's read: this needs to be caught earlier, specifically at the "Chrome isn't connected" moment — raise it immediately, pause, get Chrome started, resume, *then* check the box, rather than silently deferring the check and letting review catch the gap after the fact. Captured as Action Item #1.
2. **The real AI classification round-trip has never been exercised.** 6.3 shipped the full plumbing (port, adapter, Household toggle, background job, graceful degradation) but `NoOpAiPlausibilityClient` is the only path ever proven live — no `AiPlausibility:BaseUrl` is configured anywhere in this environment. Not a bug (AD-8's whole point is that this degrades honestly), but a real open decision: which backend (Azure AI Foundry, self-hosted LMStudio, a plain cloud API), and its cost/latency/privacy tradeoffs, still needs to be made on purpose rather than by default. Captured as Action Item #2.
3. **Story 6.1's own status flip to `done` was bundled into 6.2's diff**, coupling the two stories' lifecycle state (a 6.2 revert would have reverted 6.1's already-shipped code too, even though nothing in it changed). Process observation only, not re-opened as an action item — noted for awareness.
