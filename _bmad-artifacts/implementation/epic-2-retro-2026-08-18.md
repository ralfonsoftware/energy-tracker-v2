## What Didn't Go Well

- **Live/browser-based verification was skipped on 5 of 7 stories** ("browser-based visual verification was not performed" appears in 2.2, 2.2b, 2.3, 2.5's Debug Log References), due to Claude-in-Chrome being unavailable in the dev environment and no local OIDC bypass existing. This directly traces back to Epic 1's retro action item #1 ("verified against the real deployed environment" AC) — that item never got triggered because no Epic 2 story touched auth/ingress/deploy, but the underlying capability gap it was meant to close never got closed either, and it showed up as a recurring caveat all epic instead.
- **The `npx shadcn add` CLI alias-misresolution bug hit three separate times** (Stories 2.2, 2.2b, 2.5) — same failure (files written to a stray `web/@/components/ui/` instead of `web/src/components/ui/`), same manual fix each time, nothing durable done about it.
- **A `cva`/`tailwind-merge` class-ordering trap** in Story 2.2b: new button variant sizing (`h-11 px-6`) was silently discarded at every call site because `size`'s classes apply after `variant`'s — caught in review, not before, and only fixed via a `compoundVariants` workaround.

**Key insight:** the pattern that repeated most across Epic 2 wasn't a design or architecture gap — it was tooling/environment friction (shadcn CLI, no live-browser verification) absorbing rework that better tooling would have prevented outright, in contrast to Epic 1 where the recurring theme was a *process* gap (verification stopping short of "real deployed environment").
